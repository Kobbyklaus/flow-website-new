# How to Watch FLOW Live — in Your Language

FLOW meets online **Tuesdays & Fridays at 4:30 AM GMT**. You can now follow the
whole meeting in your own language — the words are translated for you in real time.

Currently available: **English (original), Français, Português, Español.**

---

## The simple steps (for everyone)

1. **Open the FLOW website** on your phone or computer.
2. Tap the **“WATCH LIVE”** button (top menu, or the big button on the home page).
   *(Direct link: add `/watch` to the website address.)*
3. When it’s service time, the **live video appears** in the player.
4. Under the video, you’ll see **“Choose your language.”** Tap your language —
   **English**, **Français**, **Português**, or **Español**.
5. The video now plays with that language’s audio. **Turn up your volume** (or use
   earphones) and worship along. 🙏

> **If you still hear English after choosing a language:** look at the **little
> language menu at the bottom of the video player** (it shows a small “🄰” with the
> language name). Tap it and pick your language there too. That’s the player’s own
> switch — it makes sure you get the translated sound.

That’s it. You can change language any time by tapping a different one.

---

## Good to know

- **Best experience:** use the **Chrome** or **Safari** browser, on any phone,
  tablet, or computer. Earphones give the clearest sound.
- **It’s live audio translation** — an AI voice speaks the message in your
  language a second or two after it’s spoken. This is normal.
- **Data use:** it’s a live video, so use **Wi-Fi** where you can.
- **You don’t need an account, app, or password.** Just open the page.

---

## If something isn’t working

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **“We’re not live right now”** | The meeting hasn’t started. Come back at service time (Tue/Fri 4:30 AM GMT). |
| **No sound** | Tap the video once, make sure it isn’t muted, and turn up your device volume. |
| **Still hearing English** | Use the small language menu at the **bottom of the player** and pick your language. |
| **Video keeps buffering** | Check your internet; switch to Wi-Fi; refresh the page. |
| **Nothing loads** | Refresh the page, or open it in Chrome/Safari. |
| **Want the original voice** | Tap **English (Original)**. |

## For the FLOW team (before each service)

1. Start the **Palabra Broadcaster** for the FLOW stream.
2. Make sure the **distributor is pushing the live feed to Palabra’s RTMP**
   (in addition to Vimeo/YouTube). See `PALABRA_SETUP.md`.
3. Translation goes live automatically — viewers just pick their language.
4. To add more languages later, add them in the Palabra dashboard **and** in
   `src/lib/broadcast.config.ts`, then redeploy.
